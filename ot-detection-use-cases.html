OT-DC-001Wave 1

External remote access outside authorized scope

Access and network boundaries

Identify successful external VPN or remote-support access into OT/DMZ that violates the approved account, route, target, or support authorization.

T0822
Detection design and evidence requirements

Detection logic

Join a successful gateway session to PAM/jump and destination logons; compare identity, source path, target and permitted action against the approval. Treat geography as enrichment and account for VPN/NAT.

OT-DC-003Wave 1

Authentication attack against OT access services

Access and network boundaries

Detect password guessing against one account and password spraying across multiple accounts, with a separate escalation for a subsequent successful login.

No proposed ICS mapping
Detection design and evidence requirements

Detection logic
